Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Responsiveness is a Chrome extension that shows a website in two device-sized viewports so you can compare mobile and tablet layouts.

Data we do not collect

The extension does not include analytics, advertising, account systems, or remote servers. It does not sell or share personal information.

Data stored on your computer

  • The last devices and orientations you selected (Chrome sync storage).
  • Recent URLs you opened in the tester (local storage on this browser profile).

You can clear this by removing the extension.

Site access

Optional host permission is used only so the page you choose can load in the tester frames, and so embedding headers that block iframes can be ignored for those framed previews. The extension does not read the content of your browsing outside the tester.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project repository that ships this extension.
